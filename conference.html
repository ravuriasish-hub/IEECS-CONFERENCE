About SCSVMV University

Sri Chandrasekharendra Saraswathi Viswa Mahavidyalaya was established in 1993 under Sri Kanchi Kamakoti Peetam Charitable Trust. The university is located in Enathur, Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu.

About ECE DEPARTNENT

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) focuses on providing quality education, technical knowledge and research opportunities in the fields of electronics, communication and emerging technologies. The department emphasizes both theoretical knowledge and practical skills through laboratory experiments, technical projects, workshops, seminars and industry-oriented learning.

About IEECS 2027

International Conference on Intelligent Electronics, Edge AI and Next-Generation Communication Systems. It is a proposed international technical conference focused on emerging developments in electronics, Artificial Intelligence, embedded intelligence and advanced communication technologies. The conference aims to bring together researchers, academicians, engineers, industry professionals and students to share innovative ideas, present research findings and discuss future technological developments

The conference encourages innovation, research, collaboration and the development of intelligent solutions for global challenges.
